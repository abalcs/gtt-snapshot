# Managing This Site — A Guide for the Site Manager

Welcome. This site (the GTT Country Snapshot) is built so that almost everything
on it can be changed from inside the site itself. You do not need to write code,
and you do not need to ask a developer for day-to-day changes. This guide covers
what you can do, where to do it, and the few things to leave alone.

## Signing in and roles

- Sign in with your Audley email and password. Use **Keep me signed in** on a
  computer only you use.
- **Advisors** can view everything. **Admins** can also edit. If something in
  this guide mentions a button you cannot see, you are probably signed in as an
  advisor — ask an admin to upgrade your account under **Admin → Users**.

## The Admin Dashboard

**Admin** (in the sidebar) is your home base. Every tool is a card with a short
description. Two cards deserve a glance whenever you visit:

- **Stop Sells** — a red badge means stop sells have expired and need review.
- **Feedback** — a red badge means advisors have sent suggestions or reports.

## Pages (the Guides, Resources and Tools in the sidebar)

Open **Admin → Page Manager**.

- **New Page** creates a page. Give it a title, pick a section (Guides,
  Resources or Tools), an icon, and build it from blocks: headings, text,
  callout boxes, checklists, tables, expandable sections, link cards and
  banners. The address (the bit after /p/) is made from the title.
- **Edit** opens the page builder with a live preview on the right.
- **Draft vs Published**: drafts are invisible to advisors. Work in draft, then
  publish when ready. The eye toggle in Page Manager hides a published page
  from the sidebar without unpublishing it.
- **Reorder**: use the arrows in Page Manager to change the order pages appear
  in the sidebar.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy (as a draft) — handy for a seasonal page based on
  last year's.
- **Delete** is permanent. The site will warn you, and for a published page you
  must type its address to confirm. Think twice; there is no undo for a delete.
- **Version history**: every time a page is saved, the previous version is kept.
  Open the page in the editor and scroll to **Version History** to restore an
  older version. Restoring also saves the current version first, so a restore
  can itself be undone. The last 20 versions are kept.
- Pages also appear in **site search**, so clear titles and a one-line summary
  genuinely help advisors find things.

## Site wording and the announcement banner

**Admin → Site Settings** controls:

- The home page headline and subtitle.
- The **announcement banner** — a strip at the top of every page for urgent
  notices (office closures, system outages). Turn it on, write the text, add a
  link if there is one, and remember to turn it off when it is no longer true.

## Destinations

**Admin → (dashboard) → a destination's Edit**, or search the list on the
dashboard.

- Most fields are self-explanatory; the public page shows them in sections.
- **Header Photo URL**: the banner photo at the top of the destination page.
  Paste a link to an image (a wide landscape works best) and check the preview
  in the form. Leave it blank and the page uses the plain teal banner instead.
- **Status**: Active, Not Selling, or Stop Sell. Stop sells can carry an expiry
  date — when it passes, the dashboard Stop Sells card flags it for review.
- Talking points, key facts, seasonality, pricing and client types all feed
  the destination page and the **Help Me Choose** filters, so small edits here
  have wide effects. Save, then open the public page to check.

## People

- **Admin → Users**: add accounts, change roles (advisor/admin), deactivate
  people who have left. Deactivate rather than delete.
- **Admin → Consultants**: the country specialist listings shown on the
  Specialists page.

## Pricing visibility

The **Pricing** card on the dashboard hides or shows all pricing across the
site in one switch. If advisors report prices have vanished, check this first.

## Feedback

**Admin → Feedback** collects suggestions from advisors. Mark items as handled
so the badge count stays meaningful.

## Golden rules

1. Draft first, publish second.
2. After any change, open the public page and look at it as an advisor would.
3. Deleting is forever; editing is not. When in doubt, hide or draft instead
   of deleting.
4. The banner is for things that are true *now*. Take it down when it stops
   being true.

## What not to touch

- Anything outside the Admin area that asks about code, hosting, databases or
  GitHub is not part of site management — leave it for whoever looks after the
  technical side.
- If the site itself is down or will not let anyone sign in, that is a
  technical problem, not a content problem: escalate rather than experimenting.

## If you get stuck

Check the page's **Version History** (for content mistakes), the **Activity
Log** under Admin → Operations (to see who changed what, and when), and this
guide. Most content problems are one restore away from fixed.

## Recent additions (Nov 27, 2026)

- **Dark mode**: moon/sun toggle in the header. Each person's choice is saved on their machine; first visit follows the computer's own setting. The login page keeps its fixed design in both modes.
- **Your activity**: clicking your initials in the header opens your usage stats, with a "Share my stats" button that copies a picture card you can paste into Teams.
- **Home hero**: the flag mosaic on the right of the home hero links each flag to its destination.
- **Analytics quality**: searches are recorded once when the person finishes typing (not per keystroke), Help Me Choose filter use is recorded again (see Popular Filters), and copying an email template counts in Feature Usage alongside Compare.
- **Travel Data sync**: health data now syncs in small batches with a progress count, so the buttons no longer time out. If a sync reports an error, note the message shown; the counts on each card show how many countries updated.
